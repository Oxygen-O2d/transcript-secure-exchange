## ✅ HOW TO USE THESE (IMPORTANT)

* Don’t search all at once
* Do **2–3 searches**, then take a break (10–20 min)
* Mix **learning, daily life, and curiosity**
* Click results occasionally, read an article, scroll

## ⏰ SAFE DAILY SEARCH PATTERN (EXAMPLE)

**Morning**

* Weather today
* News update

**Afternoon**

* One learning topic
* One how-to search

**Evening**

* Casual curiosity
* Tech or productivity search

👉 This avoids **rapid back-to-back searches** and keeps your account healthy.

---

## ❌ WHAT TO AVOID (YOU ALREADY KNOW, BUT IMPORTANT)

* Searching abc, 123, random letters
* Repeating similar words fast
* Opening many articles instantly
* Searching nonstop for 5–10 minutes
